The Nine-Form Wheel

All twelve Breathing Styles are nine Forms in a circle. What a Form does depends on which Form came before it — so pick a style, then click positions to build a chain and watch the Breath Count climb or break. End turn marks a round boundary: the Crest is spent once per round, and Sun's Count decays there.

A style is its topology. Read each row as "from any Form, a step of +1 … +8 does this." The density of green is how forgiving the wheel is: Thunder has one green cell and Sun has eight, and that contrast is the whole spread of the class.

Built from Docs/breath-slayer-guide-v5.md §4 and §8 — all Form text, topologies, stances, style riders and Cadence riders are read straight out of the guide, so this page and the document cannot disagree. Breath Count caps follow the chassis (2 at 1st, 4 at 5th, 6 at 9th, 9 at 13th); Thunder's cap of 7 and Sound's cap of 6 are style clauses, and both upgrade their Crest dice at that ceiling instead of at 9.
