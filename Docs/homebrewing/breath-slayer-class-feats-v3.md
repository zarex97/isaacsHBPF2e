# Breath Slayer Class Feats — Compendium v3
### Aligned with Forms Compendium v3 (patched). Replaces feat compendium v2.

**Changes in v3:**
- **Hidden Form moved from feat 18 to feat 20** — your style's Final/Forbidden Form is now the literal capstone.
- **Perfected Breath removed** entirely.
- **Multi-style ladder codified:** slayers with Flowing Stance Shift buy their secondary style's forms at **6 / 10 / 14** (Second/Third/Fourth) — always one step behind the primary ladder. There is no secondary purchase at 18: the secondary ladder ends at Fourth Form, because **no slayer ever learns a second Final Form**.

**Design rules (unchanged):** anchors cited for every feat (±2 levels); Form feats grant +1 Breath Point to a max of 3 (secondary-style Form feats do NOT grant pool — the pool caps via the primary ladder); feats sit outside the BCS 2100 budget.

## THE FORM LADDERS (reference)

| Feat | Lvl | Grants | Pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Focused Breathing** | 1 | Primary style's First Form | 1 |
| **Second Form** | 4 | Primary's second form | +1 (2) |
| **Third Form** | 8 | Primary's third form | +1 (3, cap) |
| **Fourth Form** | 12 | Primary's fourth form | — |
| **Hidden Form** | 20 | Primary's **Final/Forbidden Form** | — |

**Secondary ladder** (requires Flowing Stance Shift, feat 4):

| Feat | Lvl | Grants | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Borrowed Form** | 6 | Secondary style's First + Second Form | Flowing Stance Shift; primary Second Form |
| **Adopted Form** | 10 | Secondary's third form | Borrowed Form; primary Third Form |
| **Assimilated Form** | 14 | Secondary's fourth form | Adopted Form; primary Fourth Form |

*(Borrowed Form grants two forms because a single low-rank form at level 6 underpays the feat slot — anchor: Multifarious Muse-style versatility feats. The primary-ladder prerequisites enforce "your true style leads." No pool increases anywhere on this ladder.)*

## Level 1

**Slayer's Conditioning** — *Anchor: Fleet*
+5-foot status bonus to Speed while in a Breathing Stance and wearing no heavier than light armor.

**Demon Lore** — *Anchor: Monster Hunter (ranger 1)*
Trained in Demon Lore (or Fiend/Undead Lore, GM's call). Recall Knowledge about a fiend or undead: success also reveals one weakness or resistance; crit success also reveals its lowest save.

**Water-Wheel Footwork** — *Anchor: Nimble Dodge (rogue 1)*
**Reaction.** Trigger: targeted by a melee attack while in a stance. +2 circumstance to AC against the triggering attack.

**Deep Breath** — *Anchor: small recovery utility, feat 1 tier*
Once per 10 minutes, 1 action: temporary HP equal to half your level (min 1) for 1 minute, and an assisted flat check (DC 10) to end one persistent bleed on yourself.

---

## Level 2

**Nichirin Draw** — *Anchor: Quick Draw (rogue/ranger 2)*
Draw your nichirin blade and Strike as one action. If hidden or undetected by the target, +1d6 precision damage (once per combat).

**Slayer's Senses** — *Anchor: sense feats, investigator/rogue 2 tier*
Choose smell or hearing. Smell: imprecise scent 30 feet. Hearing: within 15 feet, dim-light concealment doesn't apply against you. Take twice (6+) for both.

**Guard Break** — *Anchor: Snagging Strike (fighter 1), taxed one level for stance synergy*
While in a stance, Strike; on a hit the target is off-guard against the next attack made against it before your next turn.

---

## Level 4

**Flowing Stance Shift** — *Anchor: versatility feats; strictly weaker than Stance Savant (monk 12)*
Learn a second Breathing Style's **stance only** (no rider, no granted skill). This opens the secondary Form ladder (Borrowed/Adopted/Assimilated Form at 6/10/14). Entering any stance costs 1 action as normal.

**Breath-Enforced Blade** — *Anchor: Ki Strike's type flexibility*
While in a stance, your nichirin Strikes can deal your style's damage type instead of the weapon's type, chosen per Strike. (With a secondary stance active, use that style's type.)

**Anticipate Lunge** — *Anchor: Stand Still (monk 4)*
**Reaction.** Trigger: a creature within reach Strides, Steps, or Flies away from you while you're in a stance. Make a nichirin Strike against it.

---

## Level 6

**Borrowed Form** — *(secondary ladder; see table above)*

**Flowing Water Counter** — *Anchor: Riposte-family feats at 6–8*
**Reaction.** Trigger: a melee Strike against you fails or critically fails while you're in a stance. Step, then Strike the triggering creature at your current MAP.

**Twin-Blade Discipline** — *Anchor: Double Slice (fighter 1), gated to 6, flourish-tagged*
With a nichirin blade in each hand while in a stance: 2 actions, Strike once with each; both use your current MAP, then MAP increases once. Flourish.

**Scent of Blood** — *Anchor: Blind-Fight (fighter 8), discounted for narrow scope*
Requires Slayer's Senses (smell). Creatures below half HP within your scent range are automatically detected unless they mask their scent.

---

## Level 8

**Rapid Concentration** — *Anchor: focus-economy feats (monk 8–12 tier)*
When you Refocus, recover 2 Breath Points if you spent 2+ since your last Refocus. *(Remaster tables where pool recovery scales automatically: replace with **Battle Breathing** — +1 circumstance to your first Form's attack roll or DC each combat.)*

**Cutting Gale** — *Anchor: heavily discounted Whirlwind (fighter 14)*
2 actions, flourish. One nichirin Strike against each of two adjacent enemies (MAP increases after both resolve).

**Unshakeable Core** — *Anchor: save-rider feats at 8*
Success on a Will save vs. fear becomes a critical success. While in any Breathing Stance, allies within 10 feet gain +1 circumstance to saves vs. fear.

---

## Level 10

**Adopted Form** — *(secondary ladder)*

**Total Concentration: Battle Trance** — *Anchor: focus-economy, monk 10–12*
Once per day when you roll initiative, regain 1 Breath Point (up to max).

**Mark Precursor: Crimson Blade** — *Anchor: Mystic Strikes chain, feat-ized*
1 action while in a stance, 1 minute: your nichirin Strikes ignore physical resistance up to half your level, and the blade counts as cold iron and silver against **all** creatures.

**Read the Opening** — *Anchor: Devise-a-Stratagem-adjacent economy*
1 action. Choose one enemy you can see: your next nichirin Strike against it this turn gains +1 circumstance (+2 if you've hit it with a Form this combat).

---

## Level 12

**Stance Savant** — *Anchor: Stance Savant (monk 12, identical)*
When you roll initiative, enter a Breathing Stance you know as a free action.

**Persistent Ember** — *Anchor: Bloody Debilitation-tier persistent riders*
Requires Flame, Thunder, or Moon style (primary or secondary). Your Forms' persistent damage dice increase one step, and a creature taking your style's persistent damage at the start of its turn is off-guard to you until the end of your next turn.

**Resonant Opening** — *Anchor: debuff-extension feats at 10–12*
Requires Sound, Flower, or Mist style (primary or secondary). When your Form makes a creature off-guard, dazzled, or deafened, the condition lasts 1 additional round (once per creature per combat).

---

## Level 14

**Assimilated Form** — *(secondary ladder)*

**Transparent World** — *Anchor: Blind-Fight fully online + info rider; upgrades the L11 chassis feature*
Your Transparent World Glimpse works continuously in a stance: ignore concealment entirely; invisible creatures are merely hidden to you. Once per round when you hit a creature, learn its remaining HP category (healthy / bloodied / near death).

**Two-Style Flow** — *Anchor: strictly weaker Fuse Stance (monk 20) precursor*
Requires Flowing Stance Shift. Once per round, swap between your two known stances as a free action.

---

## Level 16

**Selfless Guard** — *Anchor: champion-reaction/Bodyguard tier at 14–16*
**Reaction.** Trigger: an ally within your Speed is targeted by an attack you can see. Stride to the ally; if you end adjacent, you become the target instead. Once per 10 minutes.

**Breath of Endurance** — *Anchor: physical-perfection tier (monk 16)*
You age slowly, need only 4 hours of sleep, and gain +2 circumstance to Fortitude saves vs. fatigued/drained effects. At half HP or lower: resistance to bleed equal to half your level. *(Ruling: Final/Forbidden Form self-costs are unavoidable and unaffected by this feat.)*

---

## Level 18

**Awakened Mark** — *Anchor: Exalt-tier chassis upgrade*
Requires Demon Slayer Mark. At half HP or lower, your Mark manifests: you gain BOTH Mark benefits (+10-foot status Speed and the 1/day free 2-FP Form), and your Forms' status bonuses increase by 1. *(The free-Form benefit cannot be applied to a Final/Forbidden Form.)*

**Perfect Slayer's Focus** — *Anchor: Meditative Wellspring (monk 18)*
When you Refocus, regain all your Breath Points.

---

## Level 20

**Hidden Form** — *(the capstone; see Forms v3)*
Requires Fourth Form. Learn your primary style's Final/Forbidden Form (Focus 3 — your entire pool).

**Breath of the First** — *Anchor: Fuse Stance (monk 20)*
Requires Two-Style Flow. Merge your two stances into one named stance of your creation with both stances' benefits and both styles' damage types. You count as being in both styles' stances for Forms and feat prerequisites.

## Audit Summary

- **Level 20 is now a genuine choice** between the two capstone fantasies: your style's ultimate technique (Hidden Form) or transcendent dual-style mastery (Breath of the First). A slayer cannot have both — thematically clean and mechanically safe, since Fuse Stance is the level-20 anchor for each.
- **The single-style build** spends 5 feats on Forms (1/4/8/12/20) — identical to before, with the Final Form arriving two levels later than v2, at the level where Quivering Palm-tier effects belong anyway.
- **The full multi-style build** spends 4 primary Form feats + Flowing Stance Shift + 3 secondary Form feats + Two-Style Flow + Breath of the First = 10 of ~11 slots. That's deliberately expensive: eight forms across two styles plus a fused stance is the entire career, and it forgoes the Final Form. The half-committed version (Flowing Stance Shift + Borrowed Form only) costs 2 slots for two off-style tricks — fair per the Multifarious Muse anchor.
- Secondary Form feats grant no pool, so per-encounter output never exceeds the 3-FP cap regardless of forms known.
- Playtest watch: Borrowed Form's two-forms-in-one grant (confirm neither secondary First Form outclasses same-level options — Flower's Honorable Shadow Plum reaction is the strongest pick), and the 18-slot now being Mark/Focus only (two options is thin; if your table wants more, port Selfless Guard up or write a style-specific 18).
