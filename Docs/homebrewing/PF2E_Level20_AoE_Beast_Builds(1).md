# PF2e Level 20 "Beast" AoE Damage Builds
*Remaster rules (Player Core 1/2, GM Core, Rage of Elements, Dark Archive). Alchemist excluded per request.*

**Standard PF2e leveling chart used below** (same for every class): Ancestry feat at 1/5/9/13/17 · Class feat at every even level (2–20) · General feat at 3/7/11/15/19 · Skill feat at every even level · Skill increases at odd levels 3–19 · Ability boosts (4 of your choice) at 5/10/15/20.

One correction to the original report: **Pulverizing Cascade belongs to the Wave Order, not Storm.** Storm's actual advanced focus spell is **Powerful Inhalation**. Built accordingly below.

---

## 1. Kineticist — Fire Element (Single Gate → Wood as second gate)

**Key ability: Constitution.** HP is 8 + Con modifier per level, so this is the tankiest blaster on the list.

### Ability Scores (Level 20)
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 18 | **24*** | 10 | 18 | 10 |
*\*22 natural (18 at 1st level, +1 at each of 5/10/15/20) + Apex item (+2 Con).*

Dex and Wis both get pushed hard too since Kineticist has no other way to boost AC/Will — this isn't a single-stat-dump build the way a caster is.

### Feat Selection (Class feats — this is where your damage lives)
| Level | Feat/Feature | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kinetic Aura (Fire), Elemental Blast, Gather Power (class features) + 1st Fire impulse feat adding a burning/persistent-damage rider | Baseline blast + your first damage-over-time source |
| 2 | Extended Range | More impulses reach further, letting you hit backline casters with AoE |
| 4 | **Flying Flame** | Line impulse that explicitly ignores your allies — your repeatable no-slot AoE |
| 6 | Fire impulse feat that upgrades your blast's damage die size | Flat damage increase to every blast and AoE impulse |
| 8 | **Thermal Nimbus** | Stance: enemies that start their turn near you take automatic fire damage, every round, for free |
| 10 | Second-gate feat, taking **Wood** | Adds fast healing/support so you can stand in your own fire longer |
| 12 | Wood support impulse (regeneration/ally sustain) | Keeps your whole party topped off while you keep blasting |
| 14 | High-tier Fire impulse (bigger burst/persistent rider) | Scaling damage upgrade |
| 16 | Overflow-tier Fire impulse (stronger critical/persistent effect) | More damage on crits, which happen a lot with a maxed Class DC |
| 18 | Top-tier Fire AoE impulse (big burst + control, the "All-Consuming Crucible" tier) | Your closest thing to a nuke |
| 20 | **Kinetic Pinnacle** (capstone) | Extra action usable only for Channel Elements/Elemental Blast/Stance — effectively 5 actions a turn |

*Ancestry/Skill/General slots:* spend these on defense and mobility rather than damage — Toughness, Diehard, Fleet, and a Nature/Athletics skill feat like Terrain Expertise are all safe, solid picks since your class feats are doing all the damage work.

## 2. Sorcerer — Draconic Bloodline, Crystal Exemplar (Arcane)

**Key ability: Charisma.**

### Ability Scores (Level 20)
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 16 | 16 | 10 | 12 | **24*** |
*\*22 natural + Apex item (+2 Cha).*

### Feat Selection
| Level | Feat | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dangerous Sorcery** | Adds precision damage to any cantrip/spell that deals no other damage — free damage on utility spells |
| 2 | **Reach Spell** (metamagic) | Turn touch/short-range spells into ranged ones so you never have to walk into melee to land an AoE |
| 4 | **Bloodline Focus** | Regain a Focus Point once/day → an extra Dragon Breath per day |
| 6 | **Widen Spell** (metamagic) | Grows your cones and bursts — more bodies caught per cast |
| 8 | **Steady Spellcasting** | Stops getting your spell disrupted when hit while casting |
| 10 | **Bloodline Wellspring** | Recover Focus Points more reliably, feeding Dragon Breath again |
| 12 | **Crossblooded Evolution** | Swap in one spell from another bloodline to patch a gap (e.g., a control spell you're missing) |
| 14 | Signature-spell/repertoire-expansion feat | More flexibility heightening your best blasts on the fly |
| 16 | **Bloodline Resistance** or **Magic Sense** | Cheap, always-on defensive value |
| 18 | **Metamagic Mastery** | Apply two metamagic actions (e.g., Widen + Reach) for the cost of one |
| 20 | **Bloodline Perfection** (capstone) | A second 10th-rank spell slot — one more Meteor Swarm or Dragon Breath refresh per day |

**Spell repertoire priorities:** a rank-appropriate cone/burst blast early, **Chain Lightning** at rank 6, **Meteor Swarm** at rank 10.

## 3. Psychic — Oscillating Wave, Conscious Mind

**Key ability: Intelligence.**

### Ability Scores (Level 20)
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 16 | 14 | **24*** | 12 | 10 |
*\*22 natural + Apex item (+2 Int).*

### Feat Selection
| Level | Feat | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Cantrip Expansion** | More room for Produce Flame/Ray of Frost plus utility cantrips |
| 4 | **Spontaneous Ignition** (Psyche feat) | Converts half your Distant-Grasp/cantrip damage to fire and adds bonus fire per spell rank — a big Oscillating Wave damage spike |
| 6 | **Mental Balm** or comparable Psyche amp feat | Extra Amp option to diversify what Unleash Psyche empowers |
| 8 | **Psychic Beacon** (Amp feat) | Utility to strip concealment/invisibility on your main target before your nova turn |
| 10 | Focus-Point-recovery class feat | Psychic's Refocus normally caps at 1 Focus Point back; this feat (unlocked past level 10) lets you recover both, doubling your Amp economy |
| 12 | Feat that extends Unleash Psyche's duration or softens the post-Stupefied comedown | More usable rounds per activation |
| 14 | Higher-tier Amp feat for your fire/cold cantrips | Scaling damage upgrade |
| 16 | Defensive/utility Psyche feat | Shores up your weak AC/saves |
| 18 | Advanced Unleash Psyche feat | Bigger damage multiplier while unleashed |
| 20 | Capstone Psychic feat empowering Unleash Psyche/bonus Focus Point | Maximizes your one devastating nova turn per fight |

**Signature blast spell:** **Telekinetic Bombardment** — Occult's closest equivalent to Fireball, and your go-to leveled AoE to drop while Unleash Psyche is active.

## 4. Druid — Storm Order

**Key ability: Wisdom.**

### Ability Scores (Level 20)
| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 14 | 16 | 10 | **24*** | 10 |
*\*22 natural + Apex item (+2 Wis).*

### Feat Selection
| Level | Feat | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Storm Born** (automatic order feat, not chosen) | Grants the **Tempest Surge** order spell and boosts your weather spells |
| 2 | **Widen Spell** (metamagic) | Grows your cones/bursts, same logic as the Sorcerer |
| 4 | **Order Magic** | Borrow a spell from another order (e.g., a Flame Order utility spell) to patch a gap |
| 6 | **Storm Retribution** | Melee attackers who hit you take electricity damage back — free damage with zero action cost |
| 8 | **Soaring Shape** | Wild Shape into a flying form for perfect cone/line positioning |
| 10 | **Overwhelming Energy** | Your spells bypass resistance to their damage type — huge against the fire/cold/electricity-resistant monsters that wall out other blasters |
| 12 | **Dragon Shape** | Wild Shape into a dragon, gaining a breath-weapon attack as an extra AoE tool |
| 14 | **Timeless Nature** | Defensive/anti-aging feat that keeps you relevant late-game |
| 16 | **Effortless Concentration** | Sustain spells as a free action instead of spending an action every round |
| 18 | **Primal Wellspring** | Regain Focus Points through your own spellcasting — keeps Powerful Inhalation/Tempest Surge coming back |
| 20 | **Leyline Conduit** (capstone) | An extra spell slot of any rank you can cast, once per day |

**Advanced order spell:** **Powerful Inhalation** (not Pulverizing Cascade — see correction above), backed by slotted Primal blast spells at every rank.

*Alt subclass:* **Flame Order** trades the electricity theme for fire and **Combustion**, a persistent-damage focus spell that stacks well with a slotted fire blast — same feat skeleton above works, just swap Storm Born/Storm Retribution for the Flame equivalents.

## Quick comparison at level 20

| Class | Key ability | AoE style | Signature move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kineticist | Constitution | Sustained, every round, every fight | Flying Flame + Thermal Nimbus |
| Sorcerer | Charisma | Biggest single burst | Meteor Swarm / Dragon Breath |
| Psychic | Intelligence | Highest single-turn spike | Unleash Psyche + Telekinetic Bombardment |
| Druid | Wisdom | Sustained + tanky | Slotted blast + Powerful Inhalation/Combustion |

*Note: exact Kineticist and Psychic feat names above describe the confirmed mechanical effect from their source books (Rage of Elements / Dark Archive); a couple of high-level picks are described by function rather than a guaranteed-exact proper noun, since both are newer, less-documented classes. Sorcerer and Druid feat names are drawn from the Core Rulebook / Player Core line and are on firmer ground.*
