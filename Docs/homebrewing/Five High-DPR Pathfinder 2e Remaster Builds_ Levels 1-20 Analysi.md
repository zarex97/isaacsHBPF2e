# Five High-DPR Pathfinder 2e (Remaster) Builds, Level 1–20

## TL;DR
- The five builds are **Fighter (two-handed crit-fisher)**, **Magus (Starlit Span Spellstrike)**, **Barbarian (Giant Instinct)**, **Ranger (Flurry two-weapon)**, and **Rogue (Ruffian)** — each a distinct, community-validated top-DPR archetype with no repeated class.
- The **Fighter** is the single most reliable striker in the game (Expert weapon proficiency at level 1 — one rank ahead of every other class — rising to Master at 5 and Legendary at 13), while the **Giant Barbarian** deals the biggest raw per-hit numbers (+6 Rage damage at level 1) and the **Ranger** and **Rogue** win through multiple-attack-penalty (MAP) mitigation and conditional bonus damage.
- All builds are Remaster-legal (Player Core, Player Core 2, GM Core); the Magus still lives in *Secrets of Magic* (errata'd for the Remaster), and I flag one legacy-vs-Remaster spell caveat (Shocking Grasp → Thunderstrike) so you can confirm with your GM.

## Key Findings
- PF2e DPR is dominated by three levers: **proficiency/accuracy** (Fighter wins), **per-hit damage** (Barbarian wins), and **number of favorably-priced attacks / conditional riders** (Ranger, Rogue, Magus). Critical hits double damage in PF2e and trigger on beating AC by 10, so accuracy boosts convert directly into crit rate and are enormously valuable.
- The **standard fundamental-rune treadmill** everyone follows, with exact item levels and prices from GM Core pg. 236: a **striking rune is Level 4, 65 gp (2 damage dice); greater striking is Level 12, 1,065 gp (3 dice); major striking is Level 19, 31,065 gp (4 dice)**. [Demiplane](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/pathfinder2e/items/striking-rm) [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2829&Redirected=1) Weapon potency runes scale +1 (L2–4) → +2 (L10) → +3 (L16). Monster AC/HP is balanced around this progression, so keeping current is mandatory for competitive DPR.
- **Fighter** is Expert with simple and martial weapons at 1st level and is the only class Trained in Advanced weapons without spending a feat; Weapon Mastery raises it to Master at 5 and Weapon Legend grants a Legendary weapon group at 13 — always one step ahead of other martials — plus critical specialization access, making it the accuracy king.
- **Weapon Specialization** (a shared martial feature) deals 2 extra damage at Expert, 3 at Master, and 4 at Legendary; [TheDarkElf007](https://thedarkelf007.home.blog/2020/07/03/pf2-class-barbarian/) **Greater Weapon Specialization** scales this to 4/6/8. These flat bonuses apply to every Strike and are a quiet but major DPR contributor.
- **Magus** Spellstrike doubles BOTH weapon and spell damage on a critical hit, making it the best single-target "nova" burst in the game, especially when set up with Sure Strike (the Remaster rename of True Strike).

### BUILD 1 — FIGHTER: Two-Handed Crit-Fisher ("Greatpick/Greatsword Vicious Swing")

**Concept:** The Fighter is defined entirely by feats and weapon choice; with no subclass, it leans on the best weapon proficiency in the game (Expert at 1, Master at 5, Legendary at 13) to crit more often than any other class. This build maximizes single-target burst through a large-die two-handed weapon and critical specialization.

**Ancestry:** Human (Versatile heritage) — Natural Ambition grants a bonus 1st-level class feat, and the extra general feat accelerates the build. (Dwarf and Orc are strong durable alternatives.)
**Background:** Warrior, Gladiator, or Field Medic (any Strength + Con/Dex background).
**Key attributes:** Str (key, start 18) > Con > Dex 12 (basic armor) > Wis. Boost Str at 5/10/15/20 toward 22–23.
**Weapon:** **Greatpick** (d10, Fatal d12 — the best crit weapon, since Fighters crit more than anyone; the Pick critical specialization plus Fatal add huge crit damage) OR **Greatsword** (2d6, Versatile — highest floor). The Greatpick maximizes ceiling/burst, the Greatsword maximizes consistency.

**Level-by-level (class + key general/skill feats):**
| Lvl | Feat(s) |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Vicious Swing** (formerly Power Attack) *and* (via Human/Natural Ambition) **Sudden Charge** (mobility opener) |
| 2 | **Combat Grab** or **Brutish Shove**; skill feat: Intimidating Glare |
| 3 | General: Fleet; skill increase (Athletics) |
| 4 | **Knockdown** (Strike, then free Trip → off-guard) |
| 5 | Ability boosts; **Weapon Mastery (Master)** auto |
| 6 | **Furious Focus** (makes Vicious Swing's follow-up reliable) |
| 7 | Weapon Specialization (+3 at Master) auto; general feat |
| 8 | **Positioning Assault** or **Improved Knockdown** setup |
| 9 | **Combat Flexibility** auto (swap in an 8th-level feat daily) |
| 10 | **Improved Knockdown** (auto-Trip, no check) |
| 11 | general feat; skill increase |
| 12 | **Brutal Finish** (big finisher after Vicious Swing) |
| 13 | **Weapon Legend (Legendary)** auto; **Improved Combat Flexibility** |
| 14 | **Improved Reflexive Shield** / defensive pick |
| 16 | reach/positioning control feat |
| 18 | **Savage Critical** (crit on 19 with a pick — more crits) |
| 20 | **Weapon Supremacy** (auto) / capstone class feat |

Native class feature: **Reactive Strike (Attack of Opportunity)** at level 6, punishing enemy movement and casting.

**Runes/equipment progression:** +1 potency + striking weapon (Level 4, 65 gp) → greater striking (Level 12, 1,065 gp) → major striking (Level 19, 31,065 gp); potency +1 (L2–4) → +2 (L10) → +3 (L16). Armor: +1 resilient (~L5) → greater resilient (~L11) → major (~L18). Property runes: Flaming/Frost (mid), Greater versions plus **Greater Brilliant** (Level 18, bypasses three resistances) [Know Direction](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2021/12/foxs-cunning-ranking-the-runes-weapons-edition/) late. Boots of Bounding / athletic items for mobility.

**Damage rotation:**
- **Low (1–5):** Turn 1 Sudden Charge (Stride×2 + Strike) to engage; thereafter Strike + Strike (–0/–5 MAP) or Vicious Swing (2 actions, one big die) + Strike. Critical specialization (Pick: extra damage; Sword: Versatile) begins to pay off immediately thanks to your Expert-then-Master accuracy lead.
- **Mid (6–12):** Knockdown → target prone/off-guard (–2 AC, flat-footed) → follow-up Strikes crit far more often. Combat Flexibility slots situational feats daily.
- **High (13–20):** Legendary proficiency means routine crits vs on-level foes. Vicious Swing + Brutal Finish nova; Savage Critical widens crit range; Reactive Strike adds free attacks.

**Why high DPR:** The Fighter's proficiency lead (Expert at 1, +2 over baseline monster expectations at many tiers) converts directly into a higher hit AND higher crit rate than any other martial; since crits double damage, this compounds. Critical specialization effects, Knockdown-generated off-guard, and native Reactive Strike all add attacks or accuracy.

**Tactical notes:** Position to threaten with Reactive Strike; use Knockdown to soften targets for the party. You have few skills, so lean on Athletics maneuvers. A flanking partner or off-guard enabler maximizes your crit rate against high-AC bosses.

### BUILD 2 — MAGUS: Starlit Span Spellstrike (ranged nova)

**Concept:** Spellstrike channels a spell into a weapon Strike; on a critical hit BOTH weapon and spell damage are doubled — the biggest single-hit burst in the game. Starlit Span lets you Spellstrike at range, solving the Magus's crippling action-economy/positioning problem.

> **Spellstrike** (Secrets of Magic, Remaster-errata'd, Archives of Nethys): "…This counts as two attacks for your multiple attack penalty, but you don't apply the penalty until after you've completed the Spellstrike… If the spell required a spell attack roll, use your Strike's results to determine the effects of both the Strike and the spell." [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=755)

**Ruleset note:** The Magus was not reprinted in Player Core; it remains in *Secrets of Magic* but is Remaster-compatible via Paizo's Fall 2024 errata, which now also lets Spellstrike deliver save-based spells (single target). After each Spellstrike you must **recharge** (a single action, or free when you cast a 1-action conflux/focus spell). [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=755)

**Ancestry:** Elf (Dex + Int, ideal for a bow build) or Human (Natural Ambition for a second L1 feat).
**Background:** Any Dex/Int background (Field Researcher, Bandit).
**Key attributes:** Dex (key, start 18) > Int > Con; enough Str for a composite bow. (Int matters more if you rely on save-spells.)
**Weapon:** Composite Shortbow (Propulsive; avoids the longbow's Volley penalty since Magus lacks Point-Blank Stance).

**Automatic class features by level:** L1 Spellstrike, Arcane Cascade, Conflux spells, Hybrid Study (Starlit Span → Shooting Star conflux); L5 Weapon Expertise (Expert); L7 Studious Spells (bonus slots incl. **Sure Strike**), Weapon Specialization; L9 Expert Spellcaster; L11 Studious adds **Haste**; L13 Weapon Mastery (Master); L17 Master Spellcaster; L19 Double Spellstrike.

**Class feat progression:**
| Lvl | Feat |
|---|---|
| 2 | **Force Fang** (conflux spell — auto-hit 1d4+1 force AND a free recharge tool) or Cantrip Expansion |
| 4 | **Starlit Eyes** (ignore concealment on Spellstrike) |
| 6 | **Meteoric Spellstrike** / utility |
| 8 | **Fused Staff** / **Capture Magic** |
| 10 | **Rapid Recharge** (free recharge 1/day) |
| 12 | **Overwhelming Spellstrike** (ignore energy resistance = your level) |
| 14 | **Preternatural Parry** or **Hasted Assault** |
| 16 | **Dispelling Spellstrike** |
| 18 | **Conflux Wellspring** (refill all Focus Points — your recharge engine) |
| 20 | **Supreme Spellstrike** (Spellstrike no longer needs recharging — frees an action every turn) |

**Key spells:** Cantrips — **Gouging Claw** (2d6 slashing/piercing + 2 persistent bleed, doubles on crit; [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1546&Redirected=1) best because it dodges the common fire/energy resistances), Ignition (melee 2d6 fire), Ray of Frost. Conflux — **Force Fang** (auto-hit force; recharges Spellstrike). Slot spells for nova crits: Hydraulic Push, Horizon Thunder Sphere (low); Disintegrate, Scorching Ray (high). **Sure Strike** (Remaster True Strike): roll the attack twice, take higher — roughly doubles crit chance and thus the doubled-damage payoff.

> **Sure Strike** (Player Core, Archives of Nethys): "The next time you make an attack roll before the end of your turn, roll it twice and use the better result…" [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1709)

**Runes/equipment:** Bow gets +1 striking (Level 4, 65 gp) → greater striking (Level 12, 1,065 gp) → major striking (Level 19, 31,065 gp); potency scaling as normal. Staff/wands to expand slot economy. Medium armor (or light for the +10 ft Arcane Cascade speed if unarmored — less relevant at range).

**Damage rotation:**
- **Low (1–4):** Turn A: Sure Strike + Spellstrike (bow + Gouging Claw) at full attack bonus — roughly 10–11 average on a hit, ~21+ on a crit plus doubled bleed. Turn B: recharge (cast Shooting Star / Force Fang conflux for a free recharge) + Strike.
- **Mid (7–12):** Cantrips auto-heighten (Gouging Claw ~4d6 at L7); add weapon specialization, striking runes, and Haste (extra action = Spellstrike every turn). Load a leveled attack-spell for a crit spike.
- **High (17–20):** Gouging Claw ~10d6 + big bleed; load Disintegrate into a Sure Strike'd Spellstrike for 150+ crit turns. Double Spellstrike (L19) re-fires the slot spell free; Supreme Spellstrike (L20) removes the recharge tax.

**Why high DPR:** Crit-doubling of two damage sources at once is unmatched burst; Sure Strike manufactures crits; ranged delivery (Starlit Span) removes the movement tax that neuters melee maguses; MAP isn't applied until after the Spellstrike, so the big hit is always at full bonus.

**Tactical notes:** You have only two top-level slots — this is a nova class, not a sustained-DPR class; pace your big spells. Focus-point (conflux-spell) management is your recharge engine. Very "hit or miss": because Spellstrike eats your MAP, a miss largely wastes the turn, so Sure Strike and ally-provided off-guard are high-value.

### BUILD 3 — BARBARIAN: Giant Instinct (biggest per-hit numbers)

**Concept:** Giant Instinct grants the largest Rage damage bonus in the game and lets you wield an oversized (Large) weapon, producing the highest raw per-hit damage of any martial. Per Player Core 2 pg. 75, the larger-weapon Rage bonus is **+6 at 1st level** (versus the standard +2), and its instinct specialization **increases that Rage damage from 6 to 10 at 7th level, and from 10 to 18 with greater weapon specialization**. It trades some accuracy/AC for sheer damage.

**Ancestry:** Human, Orc (Orc Ferocity to stay up), or Lizardfolk. Half-Orc for both feat lists.
**Background:** Any Str + Con background.
**Key attributes:** Str (key, 18) > Con > Dex 12 > Wis.
**Weapon:** Oversized Greatsword (Large → 2d12) or oversized Greatpick for crit builds.

**Level-by-level:**
| Lvl | Feat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Sudden Charge** (Stride×2 + Strike opener) |
| 2 | **Raging Intimidation** (Demoralize → off-guard synergy); skill: Intimidating Glare |
| 4 | **Furious Vengeance** (free Strike when a foe crits you) or **Swipe** |
| 6 | **No Escape** / mobility |
| 8 | **Cleave** (kill → free adjacent Strike) |
| 10 | **Come and Get Me** (temp HP + retaliation Strikes) |
| 12 | **Vicious Evisceration** or brutal finisher |
| 14 | **Collateral Thrash** (Swipe scaling) |
| 16 | **Brutal Critical** (extra crit die) |
| 18 | **Devastating Blow** / instinct capstone feat |
| 20 | **Unstoppable Juggernaut** (auto) / capstone |

Key features: L1 Rage, Giant Instinct; L3 Deny Advantage; L7 Weapon Specialization + instinct specialization (Rage damage rises to +10); L9 Raging Resistance; L15 Greater Weapon Specialization (Rage damage rises to +18).

**Runes/equipment:** Standard striking treadmill on the oversized weapon (needs the larger-size version): striking (L4) → greater (L12) → major (L19). A **Furious rune** early synergizes with Rage. Because Giant Instinct lowers AC, prioritize resilient runes and defensive items; light/medium armor.

**Damage rotation:**
- **Low (1–5):** Rage (immediate +6 Rage damage on an oversized weapon), Sudden Charge in, then two-Strike turns. Highest floor damage of any class at this tier.
- **Mid (6–12):** Weapon spec + instinct specialization push Rage damage to +10; Cleave turns kills into free attacks; Raging Intimidation makes targets off-guard.
- **High (13–20):** Greater weapon spec pushes Rage damage to +18, plus Major Striking + Brutal Critical for enormous crit dice. Come and Get Me converts enemy attacks into your extra damage.

**Why high DPR:** Largest flat per-hit bonus in the game (Rage/instinct specialization up to +18, on oversized dice); Cleave and Furious Vengeance generate extra attacks; crit-dice scaling. The tradeoff is lower AC (you're easier to hit) — mitigated by Raging Resistance and party support.

**Tactical notes:** Giant Instinct punishes sloppy positioning because you're easier to hit, so you need party healing, flanking, and lane control. Best paired with a defender/healer. Use Intimidation to generate off-guard.

### BUILD 4 — RANGER: Flurry Two-Weapon (MAP-mitigation king)

**Concept:** The Flurry Hunter's Edge slashes your multiple-attack penalty against your Hunted Prey, letting you land 3–4 accurate attacks a turn. Per Flurry (Player Core pg. 154): "Your multiple attack penalty… is –3 (–2 with an agile attack) on your second attack… and –6 (–4 with an agile attack) on your third or subsequent attack"; [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/HuntersEdge.aspx?ID=4&Redirected=1) and once you reach **master proficiency it becomes "–2 (–1 with an agile weapon) on your second attack… and –4 (–2 with an agile weapon) on your third and subsequent attacks."** [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/HuntersEdge.aspx?ID=4&Redirected=1) Ranger is the "storm of attacks" DPR class.

**Ancestry:** Human, Elf (Dex), or Gnoll.
**Background:** Hunter, Bounty Hunter (any Dex/Str).
**Key attributes:** Dex (key, 18) > Str/Con > Wis. Two agile weapons (two shortswords, or shortsword + hatchet).

**Level-by-level:**
| Lvl | Feat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Twin Takedown** (1 action, two Strikes vs Hunted Prey) |
| 2 | **Quick Draw** / **Hunter's Aim**; skill: Assurance (Athletics) |
| 4 | **Twin Parry** (defense) or Scout's Warning |
| 6 | **Skirmish Strike** / mobility |
| 8 | **Master Monster Hunter** |
| 10 | **Twin Riposte** (reaction Strike vs your prey) |
| 12 | **Penetrating Shot** / Impossible Flurry setup |
| 14 | **Impossible Flurry** (three Strikes at low MAP) |
| 16 | advanced two-weapon feat |
| 18 | **Perfect / Legendary Shot** or advanced |
| 20 | capstone class feat |

Key features: L1 Hunt Prey + Flurry edge; L5 Weapon Expertise, Trackless; L7 Weapon Specialization; L9 Nature's Edge (foes off-guard in natural terrain); L13 Weapon Mastery (Master → Flurry MAP drops to –2/–4, and –1/–2 with agile weapons); L17 Masterful Hunter.

**Runes/equipment:** Two weapons means double rune cost — buy **Blazons of Shared Power** to share property runes across both, and **Doubling Rings** to copy fundamental runes to the off-hand weapon (the key economy item). Striking treadmill on the primary: striking (L4) → greater (L12) → major (L19).

**Damage rotation:**
- **Low (1–5):** Hunt Prey (1 action) turn 1, then Twin Takedown (two Strikes, 1 action) + another Strike. Flurry keeps the 2nd/3rd attacks accurate at –3/–6 (–2/–4 agile).
- **Mid (6–12):** Three attacks per turn at –0/–2/–4 with agile weapons; Nature's Edge grants off-guard in natural terrain.
- **High (13–20):** Master proficiency drops Flurry MAP to –1/–2 (agile) — you make 3–4 near-full-bonus attacks; Impossible Flurry adds even more Strikes. Sustained-DPR leader.

**Why high DPR:** Flurry is the best MAP mitigation in the game; more accurate attacks = more total hits and crits. Hunt Prey is cheap action economy, and agile off-hand weapons stack the MAP reduction. Consistent, sustained (not nova) damage.

**Tactical notes:** Re-target Hunt Prey each fight (or take Double Prey). Two-weapon builds are action-hungry — plan your Hunt Prey economy. Weaker crit specialization than the Fighter; wins on volume, not per-hit size.

### BUILD 5 — ROGUE: Ruffian (Strength melee sneak-attacker)

**Concept:** Sneak Attack adds precision damage whenever the target is off-guard (flat-footed); the Ruffian racket lets you deal Sneak Attack with any simple weapon (and d8 martial / d6 advanced weapons), enabling a Strength-based frontline rogue with medium armor. Rogues also get more Skill Increases and Skill Feats than any class.

**Ancestry:** Human, Half-Orc, or Dwarf (durability).
**Background:** Criminal, Bandit, Prisoner (Str/Con).
**Key attributes:** Str (key for Ruffian) > Con > Dex 14 (medium armor) > Cha/Int.
**Weapon:** A d8 simple weapon still qualifies for Sneak Attack under Ruffian (e.g., a spear or a one-hander + shield). Ruffian caps Sneak Attack eligibility at d8 simple / d6 martial. [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rackets.aspx?ID=7&Redirected=1)

**Level-by-level:**
| Lvl | Feat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **You're Next** (Intimidate synergy) or **Twin Feint**; Sneak Attack 1d6 auto |
| 2 | **Brutal Beating** (Ruffian: frightened on crit) / skill feat |
| 4 | **Dread Striker** (frightened foes are off-guard to you — self-enables Sneak Attack) |
| 6 | **Twist the Knife** (persistent bleed) |
| 8 | **Sneak Savant** |
| 9 | **Debilitating Strike** auto (apply a debuff on a Sneak Attack hit) |
| 10 | **Precise Debilitations** (make target off-guard / +2d6) |
| 12 | **Vicious Debilitations** |
| 14 | **Critical Debilitation** (slow the target on a crit) |
| 16 | advanced racket feat |
| 18 | **Dispelling Slice** / capstone feat |
| 20 | **Master Strike** (auto) / capstone |

Key features: Sneak Attack scales 1d6 (L1) → 2d6 (L5) → 3d6 (L11) → 4d6 (L17); L5 crit specialization + Expert weapons; L7 Weapon Specialization; L9 Debilitating Strike; L13 Master weapon proficiency, Improved Evasion; L15 Greater Weapon Specialization.

**Runes/equipment:** Striking treadmill (striking L4 → greater L12 → major L19). Because precision damage doesn't multiply attacks, a bigger single weapon die matters, but Ruffian caps at d8. A **Wounding rune** synergizes with Twist the Knife's bleed. Armor: resilient runes; stealth/mobility items.

**Damage rotation:**
- **Low (1–5):** Get off-guard via flanking (partner), Feint, or Demoralize (You're Next / Dread Striker). Every Sneak Attack adds 1d6→2d6. Setup action + Strike, or Strike + Strike when flanking.
- **Mid (6–12):** Dread Striker (frightened = off-guard) makes Sneak Attack reliable even solo; Debilitating Strike (L9) applies off-guard/slow debuffs on hit; Twist the Knife adds persistent bleed.
- **High (13–20):** 4d6 Sneak Attack + Greater Weapon Specialization + Major Striking; stacked debilitations lock down bosses. Master Strike can incapacitate on a failed save.

**Why high DPR:** Sneak Attack is a large, reliable per-hit precision rider that applies to every qualifying Strike; Debilitating Strike converts damage into ongoing debuffs/off-guard, feeding its own future Sneak Attacks. Ruffian's Strength chassis raises weapon damage and survivability over a Dex Thief.

**Tactical notes:** Rogue is the most team-dependent DPR class — you need consistent off-guard (flanking partner, or self-generate via Intimidation/Feint). Best-in-class out-of-combat skill utility offsets a slightly lower combat ceiling than Fighter/Barbarian. Position for flanks; use Debilitating Strike to help the whole party.

## Recommendations
1. **Most reliable, beginner-friendly DPR:** play the **Fighter** — its accuracy lead means it performs well in any party and against any AC, with the fewest moving parts.
2. **Burst/spike damage and a gish fantasy:** play the **Starlit Span Magus** — but only if your party can set up off-guard and you're comfortable managing a nova resource. Confirm with your GM whether Shocking Grasp (legacy) or Thunderstrike (Remaster) is in use for Spellstrike.
3. **Biggest raw numbers, and you don't mind risk:** play the **Giant Barbarian** — bring a healer and a flanking buddy; you'll be easier to hit.
4. **Consistent sustained damage with many attacks:** play the **Flurry Ranger** — buy Doubling Rings and Blazons of Shared Power early to afford runes on both weapons.
5. **Damage plus best-in-class skills:** play the **Ruffian Rogue** — coordinate flanking with an ally and lean on Dread Striker to self-enable Sneak Attack.

**Benchmarks/thresholds that change these picks:**
- If your party **lacks off-guard enablers** (no flankers, no debuffers), downgrade Rogue and Magus and favor Fighter/Barbarian, which don't depend on it.
- In a **Free Archetype** game, every build improves; a Fighter Dedication on any of them adds Reactive Strike and bonus feats — a strong universal pick.
- If your GM uses **Automatic Bonus Progression**, ignore the rune-treadmill costs (the numeric bonuses are granted automatically), which frees gold for property runes and utility items.
- Against **very high-AC solo bosses**, accuracy matters most — the Fighter and any build that reliably generates off-guard (Rogue/Barbarian via Intimidation, Ranger via terrain) pull ahead; pure volume builds suffer more from MAP.

## Caveats
- **The Magus is legacy-hosted:** it lives in *Secrets of Magic* (2021) with Remaster errata rather than in a Player Core book; RPGBOT's Magus ratings predate a full Remaster update and reference some old spell names. Shocking Grasp was replaced by **Thunderstrike** (a save-based spell) in the Remaster; verify which your table allows, since it changes the crit-doubling math of the Magus nova.
- **DPR figures here are illustrative approximations** built from weapon/spell dice, not quoted from a published DPR spreadsheet; actual numbers depend on target AC, buffs (Haste, Bless, off-guard), and party support. Community damage calculators (e.g., the PF2 DPR calculator by Bahalbach) can model exact curves.
- **Feat lists are optimized recommendations, not the only valid path.** Fighter Combat Flexibility and Ranger/Rogue/Magus retraining let you adapt daily or between sessions, and exact feat names/levels can vary slightly between Player Core printings and legacy references. Where a level row is blank in a table, take a defensive, utility, or ancestry feat to taste.
- **Tier-list sources cited for directional consensus** (community TierMaker rankings, Reddit/forum threads) are subjective and aggregated; they informed class selection but are not authoritative DPR proof. The mechanical claims (proficiency levels, Rage/Flurry numbers, rune levels/prices, Spellstrike and Sure Strike text) are sourced to Archives of Nethys and the Player Core / Player Core 2 / GM Core rules.
