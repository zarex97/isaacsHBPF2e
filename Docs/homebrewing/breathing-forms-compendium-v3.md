# Breathing Forms Compendium v3 — Canon Ladders & Final Forms
### Replaces v2. Nine Breathing Styles: 4 canon forms + 1 unique Final/Forbidden Form each.

## Structural changes from v2

1. **Every style has exactly 4 forms + 1 Final Form.** All forms use canon names and effects wherever canon provides them (exceptions flagged ⚠).
2. **The Thirteenth Form is retired.** In its place, the feat-20 **Hidden Form** feat unlocks your style's unique **Final Form** (or **Forbidden Form**, styles marked †) — each with its own capstone mechanic and self-cost, budgeted at the Quivering Palm (monk 16) / Impossible Flurry (ranger 18) tier.
3. **Final Forms cost 3 Focus Points** — your entire pool. Casting one is the dramatic climax of a fight by design.
4. New feat ladder (replaces v2's tier bundles):

| Feat | Level | Grants | Pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focused Breathing | 1 | Your style's **First Form** | 1 |
| Second Form | 4 | Style's second listed form | +1 (2) |
| Third Form | 8 | Style's third listed form | +1 (3, cap) |
| Fourth Form | 12 | Style's fourth listed form | — |
| **Hidden Form** | 20 | Style's **Final/Forbidden Form** | — |

Multi-style slayers (Flowing Stance Shift line) may buy a second style's Form feats but may never learn a second Final Form — a slayer perfects only one style's ultimate technique.

**Unchanged rules:** uncommon + slayer traits, Class DC as spell DC, stance + nichirin required, automatic heightening by spell rank, incapacitation on hard control, feats outside the BCS 2100 budget. All spells anchored as noted; v2's rebalancing conventions (no damage doubling; situational gates instead of multipliers) still apply.

# THE ORIGINAL FIVE STYLES

## WATER BREATHING
*Stance/rider/skill: unchanged from the class guide (Acrobatics; +1 circ AC when you moved this turn).*

**First Form: Water Surface Slash** — Feat 1, Focus 1, 1 action
Nichirin Strike, +1 status to the roll, +1d6 slashing; d8s if you Strode this turn first. **H(+4):** +1 die.
**Second Form: Water Wheel** — Feat 4, Focus 1, 2 actions, flourish
Stride without triggering reactions; Strike at any point; off-guard target if you moved 10+ feet first. **H(6th):** +1d6; **(9th):** +2d6.
**Fourth Form: Striking Tide** — Feat 8, Focus 1, reaction
Trigger: hit by a physical Strike in stance. Reduce damage by 2 + level; if attacker is adjacent, riposte Strike at MAP.
**Sixth Form: Whirlpool** — Feat 12, Focus 2, 2 actions
5-foot emanation, 6d6 slashing (basic Reflex); crit fails off-guard until your next turn; swimming/underwater targets can't Step away. **H(+1):** +1d6.

**FINAL FORM — Eleventh Form: Dead Calm** — Feat 20, Focus 3, 1 action *(Giyu Tomioka's personal creation)*
Duration: until your next turn. Enter perfect stillness: +2 status AC, and every melee Strike that misses you (max 4) triggers an automatic riposte nichirin Strike at your current MAP (each raises MAP). If you Stride, cast, or take any action other than these ripostes, the form ends instantly. **Self-cost:** when the form ends you're off-guard until the end of your next turn (the stillness must break).

# THE FOUR EXPANDED STYLES (from the 5e module)

Each new style is a full subclass: **granted skill + stance + 30-pt rider** (Exemplary Finisher anchor, BCS-neutral — subclasses are sidegrades, so the chassis ledger is untouched) + 4 forms + Final Form.

## SOUND BREATHING ⚠
*Only three Sound forms are canon (First, Fourth, Fifth); the ⚠ forms below are adapted from the 5e module's inventions.*

**Skill:** Stealth (shinobi training). **Damage type:** sonic.
**Stance — Resonant Tempo:** While in this stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Feint checks and can Feint targets within 15 feet using the sound of your blades.
**Style rider:** When a creature critically fails a save against your Form or you critically hit with one, it's **deafened** until the end of your next turn, and it's off-guard to you while deafened this way. *(Anchor: Exemplary Finisher, 30 pts.)*

**First Form: Roar** — Feat 1, Focus 1, 1 action
Strike with a thunderous impact: +1 status to the roll, +1d6 sonic; on a crit the target is pushed 5 feet by the shockwave. **H(+4):** +1d6.
**⚠ Second Form: Bang** — Feat 4, Focus 1, 2 actions
Slam both blades: 10-foot emanation of concussive sound, 2d8 sonic (basic Fortitude); crit fails are deafened 1 round. **H(+1):** +1d8.
**⚠ Third Form: Smoke** — Feat 8, Focus 1, 2 actions
Detonate a shinobi charge: 10-foot burst within 30 feet, 2d6 sonic + 1d6 fire (basic Reflex), and the area is concealing smoke until the start of your next turn. **H(+2):** +1d6 sonic.
**Fourth Form: Constant Resounding Slashes** — Feat 12, Focus 2, 2 actions, flourish
Two Strikes vs. one creature (MAP applies); each hit adds 1d6 sonic, and if both hit, the ringing leaves the target **stupefied 1** until the end of your next turn (its concentration shatters). **H(+2):** +1d6 per Strike.

**FINAL FORM — Fifth Form: String Performance** — Feat 20, Focus 3, 3 actions, flourish
A whirling, music-scored massacre: Stride up to your Speed; make one Strike each against up to three creatures adjacent to any point of your path (MAP increases after the first). Each hit deals +2d6 sonic; each creature that is hit twice— *(only possible vs. two targets or fewer)* —must succeed at a Fortitude save or be stunned 1 (**incapacitation**). **Self-cost:** the score demands everything — you're deafened for 1 minute and enfeebled 1 until the end of your next turn.

## FLOWER BREATHING
*Canon provides Second, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and a literal Final Form — a perfect fit.*

**Skill:** Medicine (Butterfly Mansion training). **Damage type:** slashing (precision-flavored).
**Stance — Graceful Bloom:** While in this stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls and to Seek.
**Style rider:** The first time each round you Strike a creature that is off-guard to you, you deal +1 precision damage per weapon damage die. *(Anchor: Precise Strike-lite at Exemplary Finisher value, 30 pts.)*

**Second Form: Honorable Shadow Plum** — Feat 1, Focus 1, reaction
Trigger: you're targeted by a melee Strike. Whirling defensive slashes: +2 circumstance AC vs. the trigger; if it misses, your next Strike against that creature before your next turn gains +1 circumstance. *(A canon defensive form as the entry point — Flower plays as the counter-duelist.)*
**Fourth Form: Crimson Hanagoromo** — Feat 4, Focus 1, 1 action
A single curving slash from an unpredictable angle: Strike with +1 status; the target is off-guard against this Strike if it has already attacked you this combat. **H(+4):** +1d6 slashing.
**Fifth Form: Peonies of Futility** — Feat 8, Focus 1, 2 actions, flourish
Nine blossoming arcs compressed into two Strikes vs. one creature (MAP applies); if either crits, the target takes 1d8 persistent bleed. **H(+2):** +1d6 per Strike.
**Sixth Form: Whirling Peach** — Feat 12, Focus 2, 2 actions
Spin past a foe: Stride up to half your Speed through or around one creature's reach without triggering its reactions, Strike it with +1 status, and it's off-guard to your allies until the start of your next turn. **H(+1):** +1d6.

**FINAL FORM — Equinoctial Vermilion Eye** † — Feat 20, Focus 3, 1 action *(canon's named Final Form — the forbidden vision technique)*
Duration: 1 minute. Your kinetic vision sharpens to the edge of human limits: you gain a +2 status bonus to AC, Reflex saves, and attack rolls; you ignore concealment entirely; and enemies are off-guard to you on your first Strike against them each round. **Self-cost (forbidden):** when the duration ends you are **blinded** until you Refocus, then dazzled for 1 hour. Each time you use this form after the first in a single day, the blindness instead persists for 24 hours — canonically, this technique costs the user their eyesight; GMs may enforce permanent consequences for habitual use.

## MIST BREATHING
*All seven Mist forms are canon; four selected + Muichiro's personal Seventh as the Final Form.*

**Skill:** Stealth. **Damage type:** slashing (obscuring flavor).
**Stance — Veiled Sky:** While in this stance, you don't take the flat check penalty for targeting concealed creatures, and you can Hide while only concealed by your own Forms' effects.
**Style rider:** Areas of mist, smoke, or obscurement your Forms create grant concealment to you but not against you. *(Anchor: Exemplary Finisher-tier tactical hook, 30 pts.)*

**First Form: Low Clouds, Distant Haze** — Feat 1, Focus 1, 1 action
Strike, +1 status, +1d6 slashing; hit = a curl of mist clings to the target: it's dazzled until the start of your next turn. **H(+4):** +1d6.
**Second Form: Eight-Layered Mist** — Feat 4, Focus 1, 2 actions, flourish
Two layered Strikes vs. one creature (MAP applies); the second Strike ignores the target's circumstance bonuses to AC (it can't read the layers). **H(+2):** +1d6 per Strike.
**Fifth Form: Sea of Clouds and Haze** — Feat 8, Focus 1, 2 actions
Blanket a 10-foot burst (within 15 feet) in mist until the start of your next turn (concealment; not against you, per rider); creatures in it when cast take 2d6 slashing (basic Reflex). **H(+2):** +1d6.
**Sixth Form: Lunar Dispersing Mist** — Feat 12, Focus 2, 2 actions
A retreating spray of slashes: Strike up to two adjacent creatures (MAP after the first), then Step twice; the mist of your passage makes you concealed until the start of your next turn.

**FINAL FORM — Seventh Form: Obscuring Clouds** — Feat 20, Focus 3, 2 actions *(Muichiro Tokito's personal creation)*
Duration: 1 minute. Dense mist erupts in a 20-foot emanation that moves with you: all creatures inside are concealed (hidden to observers outside), except that no creature is ever concealed *from you*. While the cloud lasts, once per round after you Strike, you can Step or Hide as a free action. **Self-cost:** sustaining the vast cloud drains you — when it ends, you're fatigued. *(Anchor: an upgraded, mobile obscuring mist owned by a martial — priced at full pool + fatigue.)*

## Balance Verification Summary (v3)

- **Final Forms** all sit at the Quivering Palm/Impossible Flurry tier: 3 FP (whole pool) + a real self-cost + (for hard control) incapacitation. None outputs more than a fighter's best level-18 turn; each style's Final Form expresses its identity (counter-state, nova charge, mega-mobility, root-tank, multi-target, transcendent buff, battlefield control, AoE devastation).
- Per-encounter output vs. v1/v2 is *lower or equal*: 4 forms + Final ≤ v2's 6–8 known, and the pool cap (3) is unchanged.
- New subclasses use the same 30-pt rider anchor as the original five, so the **BCS 2100 ledger from the class guide is completely unchanged** — Sound/Flower/Mist/Moon are sidegrades, not additions to the budget.
- **Playtest watch list:** Equinoctial Vermilion Eye (a 1-minute +2-status omnibuff is the strongest single effect in the class — the blindness cost must actually bite, so GMs should not hand out easy blindness removal), Dead Calm (riposte count now capped at 4), and Moonbow's persistent-bleed AoE at tables with many-enemy encounters.
