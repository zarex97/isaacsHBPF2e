# The Saint — Class Feat Compendium

*Full rules text for every Saint feat. Feats are unpriced in the BCS, so this list is generous by design — the class lives on breadth. Each entry gives level, traits, prerequisites, action cost, and effect in PF2e feat format. The eight that previously read "\[Needs development\]" are fully written here and marked ✎.*

---

## Level 1

### Cosmo Sense — Feat 1

**Traits** Saint, concentrate, divination By reading the microcosm of a creature, you gauge the macrocosm it can call on.

> **Cosmo Sense** ✦ (single action) Attempt a check to Recall Knowledge about a creature within 60 feet, using your key ability instead of the relevant skill. On a success you learn its level relative to yours (higher, equal, or lower) and its single highest and lowest saving throws; on a critical success you also learn its current focus/spell resources or that it has none. This is a fortune-free, magic-based sense — it works through disguise and illusion, reading the cosmo beneath.

### Pandora Box — Feat 1 ✎

**Traits** Saint Your dismissed Cloth is not merely stored — it is *sent*, and it can find you again across any distance.

> When you dismiss your Cloth, you may send it to a pocket of folded space instead of collapsing it into a box or your body. While there it is beyond reach of any effect. As an activity taking **10 minutes**, you may summon it to your current location from anywhere **on the same plane**; if you are on a different plane, it arrives after **24 hours**, traveling the dimensional roads on its own.  
>   
> Additionally, your Cloth can act as a courier: you may send it away carrying a single object of Bulk 2 or less, name a destination you have visited, and it will travel there and back over 24 hours, then return to you. A Saint's Cloth cannot be intercepted, tracked, or compelled while traveling — only destroyed, and only by something that can catch it.

### Saint's Vow — Feat 1 ✎

**Traits** Saint, emotion, mental You swear to protect one person, and the oath sharpens you against whatever threatens them.

> Choose one ally when you make your daily preparations; this is the target of your Vow until your next preparations. You gain a **\+1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls** against any creature that has damaged, or attempted to inflict a condition on, the target of your Vow since the end of your last turn.  
>   
> Additionally, once per round when the target of your Vow would be reduced to 0 Hit Points while you are within 30 feet, you may use a reaction to **Stride up to your Speed toward them**; if this brings them within your reach, they instead remain at 1 Hit Point (this benefit can aid a given ally only once per hour).  
>   
> **Special** You may have only one Vow at a time. Breaking a Vow deliberately — abandoning the person you swore to protect — costs you the use of this feat until you complete a full day of atonement.

### Fists of the Constellation — Feat 1 ✎

**Traits** Saint You channel your Signature Technique through your fists, letting its essence bleed into every strike.

> Choose one damage type dealt by your Cloth's Signature Technique (for example, electricity for Leo, cold for Aquarius, force for the default). Your unarmed Strikes deal that damage type instead of bludgeoning, and they count as your Signature Technique's element for the purpose of your own feats and boons that reference it.  
>   
> Once per round, when you critically hit with an unarmed Strike, the target also takes **persistent damage of that type equal to your number of weapon damage dice** (so 2 at levels 1–4, matching your striking rune thereafter).  
>   
> **Special** If your Cloth's Signature Technique deals no damage (Gemini, Virgo), you instead choose force, and your unarmed Strikes gain the effect that best matches your Cloth's theme at the GM's discretion (Virgo: on a critical hit, the target is dazzled 1 round; Gemini: on a critical hit, the target is off-guard to you until the end of your next turn).

## Level 2

### Heated-Up Cosmo — Feat 2

**Traits** Saint, concentrate You stoke your inner cosmo in an instant rather than through long meditation.

> Once per day, you may **Refocus as a single action** instead of over 10 minutes. You can do this in the middle of an encounter; the burst of raw cosmo is visible as a flare of light around you, and any creature with a Sixth Sense–equivalent ability immediately knows your location.

### Cloth Bond — Feat 2

**Traits** Saint The runes worked into your Cloth resonate with a technique you have mastered.

> Choose one Technique you know. The **fundamental and property runes on your Cloth** (potency, striking, and any property runes) also apply to that Technique's spell attack rolls and damage, treating the Technique as though it were a weapon bearing those runes. If the Technique makes a spell attack, it now adds your Cloth's potency bonus to that attack roll (removing the usual −2 gap between Strikes and Techniques for this one Technique only).  
>   
> You may retrain this feat to choose a different Technique whenever you gain a level.

## Level 4

### Rise — Feat 4

**Traits** Saint A Gold Saint does not stay down.

> **Frequency** once per day **Trigger** You are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright. **Effect** You remain conscious with 1 Hit Point instead of falling. You are **wounded 1** (or increase your wounded value by 1\) and cannot use this feat again until you have rested. This is a raw refusal to fall, not healing — if the same hit would deal enough to kill you outright (Hit Points below the negative of your maximum), Rise does not save you.

### Second Cloth Technique — Feat 4

**Traits** Saint You have studied a sister Cloth's art.

> Learn one Signature Technique from a Cloth other than your own. Its rank is **one lower** than your usual Technique rank (minimum rank 1), and it uses your Cosmo DC and focus pool normally. You may retrain which Technique this is when you gain a level. You cannot select a Cloth's L11 Cloth Ability this way — only its Signature Technique.

## Level 6

### Speed of Sound — Feat 6

**Traits** Saint When the stars align for you, you move faster than the eye can follow.

> Your Speed increases by **10 feet**. On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant** (or Exalted), this bonus increases to **\+20 feet**, and you ignore difficult terrain that isn't magical.

### Burning Cosmo  — Feat 6 ✎

**Traits** **Traits** Saint, emotion, fear, mental. You let your cosmo blaze until lesser wills buckle under the sheer weight of it. You do not threaten — you simply *are*, and it is too much.

> You radiate your cosmo as a palpable force. You can **Demoralize** using your **key ability modifier** in place of Intimidation (you are trained in this use even if untrained in Intimidation, and add your Intimidation proficiency if it is higher). When you Demoralize this way, you may target any creature within **60 feet** that can see you, ignoring the normal 30-foot range and the shared-language requirement — the pressure of an ignited cosmo needs no words.  
>   
> On a critical success, the target is **frightened 2** instead of frightened 2/1, and if it is of a lower level than you it cannot reduce its frightened value below 1 while it remains within 60 feet of you (until it succeeds against this effect or the encounter ends).  
>   
> **Special** On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant**, your cosmo is bright enough that you may Demoralize **all enemies in a 30-foot emanation** with a single action, rolling once against each. A creature that critically succeeds is temporarily immune for 10 minutes as normal.  
>

## Level 8

### Twin Cosmo — Feat 8

**Traits** Saint, concentrate You split your focus, unleashing a technique in the same breath as your defense.

> **Frequency** once per day **Trigger** An enemy takes an action that would trigger a reaction you could make, or an ally within 30 feet is reduced to 0 Hit Points. **Effect** You cast one Technique you know that has a casting time of 2 actions or fewer, as a reaction. If the Technique normally costs a Focus Point, it still does.

### Read the Constellation — Feat 8

**Traits** Saint, divination, prediction You learn to feel your own constellation approaching over the horizon of days.

> **Frequency** once per week During your daily preparations, you learn whether your Cloth's constellation will be **ascendant on any of the next three days**, and if so, which day and whether it will be Exalted. This lets you plan — to press for a confrontation on your day, or to counsel delay until the sky turns. You still learn nothing about the other twelve signs.

## Level 10

### Cosmo Overflow — Feat 10

**Traits** Saint, concentrate. Your inner cosmo runs over its banks. On the days the sky ignores you, you compensate through sheer output; on the days it favors you, you become a furnace.

> You gain the following action. **Cosmo Overflow** ✦✦ (two actions) — **Frequency** once per hour You cast a Technique you know of 2 actions or fewer **without spending a Focus Point**. On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant** (or Exalted), the frequency increases to **once per 10 minutes**, and the Technique cast this way is treated as one rank higher (to a maximum of your normal maximum rank).  
>   
> This lets a Saint who is having an ordinary day still open a fight with a Technique without draining their small pool, and lets a Saint who is having *their* day pour Techniques out at a furnace rate.

### Cosmo Legend — Feat 10

**Traits** Saint Your techniques burn at the full furnace of your cosmo, not half.

> Your Techniques' rank is equal to **half your level rounded up as normal, plus 1** (to a maximum of rank 10). In effect, your Techniques heighten one step ahead of the standard focus-rank schedule — a level-11 Saint casts as rank 7 rather than rank 6\. This applies to every Technique you know, including Cloth Abilities and boon-granted techniques.  
>   
> *(Balance note: this is a deliberately strong capstone-tier feat. Against the rank spine it advances every damage Technique by one heightening step — roughly \+15% to your Technique output. It is priced at level 10 to sit alongside comparable martial power feats and should be watched in play.)*

## Level 12

### Second Ascension — Feat 12

**Traits** Saint, concentrate For a moment, by sheer will, you make the sky obey.

> **Frequency** three times per week **Effect** For **1 minute**, treat the day as though your Cloth's constellation were **ascendant**, gaining your Ascendant Boon. If your sign is *already* ascendant (but not Exalted), you may instead spend this to treat the day as **Exalted** for 1 minute, gaining your Zenith Boon. You cannot use Second Ascension and Seventh Sense's *Burn Your Cosmo* on the same turn.

### Cloth Resonance — Feat 12

**Traits** Saint. Your bond with your Cloth has deepened past armor into a second body. Its runes, its history, and your cosmo have become one thing, and it answers your intent before your mind forms it.

> Choose two benefits from the list below; you gain both. You may retrain to swap one benefit whenever you gain a level.  
> 

> - **Reactive Cloth** — Your Cloth's AC bonus applies even in the brief instants you are flat-footed by surprise; you are never off-guard during the first round of combat while you wear it.  
> - **Runic Echo** — The property runes on your Cloth (not just fundamental) also apply to one Technique of your choice, as *Cloth Bond* but for property runes; if you have *Cloth Bond*, it now covers a second Technique as well.  
> - **Self-Mending** — When your Cloth would crack (you drop to 0 HP), it does not; instead you gain temporary Hit Points equal to your level. This can occur once per encounter.  
> - **Cosmo Conduit** — Once per day, when you Refocus, you regain **2 Focus Points** instead of 1\.

>   
> **Special** On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant**, you gain the benefit of **all four** options for that day.

>

## Level 14  Rising Cosmo — Feat 14

**Traits** Saint Every Saint learns the same truth on the edge of defeat: the more you are hurt, the brighter you burn. As the fight turns against you, your cosmo climbs — and the wounds that should end you become the fuel that answers back.

> Your cosmo intensifies as an encounter wears on you. You gain a **rising bonus** that starts at 0 and increases by 1 each time you are critically hit, reduced to half your Hit Points or lower for the first time, or reduced to 0 Hit Points (to a maximum bonus of \+3). The rising bonus resets to 0 at the end of the encounter or after a 10-minute rest.  
>   
> While your rising bonus is at least \+1, you gain that value as a **status bonus to your unarmed Strike damage and to the DCs of your Techniques**. While it is at least \+2, you also gain a **\+1 status bonus to attack rolls and saving throws**. While it is at \+3, once per round you may **Step or Stride 5 feet as a free action** when you take damage — the cosmo will not let you simply stand and fall.  
>   
> **Special** On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant** (or Exalted), your rising bonus starts each encounter at **\+1** rather than 0 — your cosmo is already lit before the first blow lands.  
> 

### Speed of Light — Feat 14

**Traits** Saint, concentrate You cross the distance between two heartbeats.

> **Frequency** once per day **Effect** You are **quickened 1 for 1 minute**. You can use the extra action each round only to Strike, Stride, or Step. While quickened this way, you also don't trigger reactions that are based on your movement (you are simply not where the reaction expected you to be).

## Level 16

### Zenith Reach — Feat 16

**Traits** Saint The awakened seventh sense answers you more than once.

> You may use Seventh Sense's **Burn Your Cosmo** action **twice per day** instead of once. Additionally, when you use it, its duration increases from 1 minute to **1 minute per Focus Point you currently have** (so up to 3 minutes).

### Atomic Dissolution — Feat 16

**Traits** Saint, concentrate Camus taught it plainly: to freeze a thing, still its atoms; to destroy a thing, part them. Every technique you have ever thrown was this, and now you do it deliberately, with your hands, to anything at all.

> You perceive and manipulate matter at its smallest scale. You gain the following benefits:  
> 

> - **Your unarmed Strikes ignore an object's Hardness entirely**, and you can damage and destroy objects and structures of any material with your bare hands, including those normally immune to nonmagical damage.  
> - **Your unarmed Strikes and Techniques treat any creature's resistance to your damage as 5 lower** (this stacks with, and applies before, the Seventh Sense's resistance-ignoring at 15th level).  
> - You can attempt to affect a creature or object that is **incorporeal, gaseous, liquid, or otherwise diffuse** — a swarm, an air elemental, a creature in *gaseous form* — with your unarmed Strikes at no penalty, and such creatures do not gain their usual resistance to physical attacks against you.

>   
> **Dissolve** ✦✦ (two actions, concentrate) — **Frequency** once per 10 minutes Touch one object of up to 10 Bulk that is not attended by a creature, or a 10-foot cube of a structure. It is **destroyed**, its atoms parted and scattered as a fine glittering dust. This cannot destroy artifacts, Cloths, or objects of similar significance, and against a creature's attended object the creature may attempt a Reflex save against your Cosmo DC to pull it clear.  
>   
> **Special** On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant** (or Exalted), *Dissolve* has no frequency limit, and its size limit increases to 40 Bulk or a 20-foot cube.  
>

## Level 18

### Entrust Your Cosmo — Feat 18

**Traits** Saint, concentrate, emotion This is the oldest thing a Saint knows how to do. When your own road ends, or when someone else's matters more, you give them what you have left — and they carry it further than you could.

> **Entrust Your Cosmo** ✦✦ (two actions) **Frequency** once per hour **Target** one willing ally you can touch, or within 30 feet if your Cloth's constellation is ascendant **Effect** You pour a portion of your cosmo into another. Spend any number of Focus Points; the target gains the following benefits for **1 minute**, scaling with the number spent:  
> 

| Focus spent | The target gains |
| :---- | :---- |
| **1** | A **\+1 status bonus to attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks**, and temporary Hit Points equal to your level. |
| **2** | As above, and their Strikes count as **magical** and deal **\+1d6 damage** of your Cloth's element. |
| **3** | As above, and they become **quickened 1** (Strike, Stride, or Step only), and they can see and act normally through any darkness, invisibility, or concealment for the duration. |

>   
> While the duration lasts you are **enfeebled 1** and cannot Refocus — you gave that away, and it does not come back until the fire returns to you.  
>   
> **The Last Gift.** If you use Entrust Your Cosmo while you are **dying**, or as a free action triggered by being reduced to 0 Hit Points, you may spend all remaining Focus Points at once and treat the result as though you had spent **3**, regardless of how many you actually had. The duration becomes **10 minutes**, and the target also gains a **\+2 status bonus to all damage rolls**. You immediately become **dying 3** — but every Saint who has done this has known exactly what it cost, and done it anyway.  
>   
> **Special** A creature can benefit from Entrust Your Cosmo only once per hour, no matter how many Saints offer it. Three Saints cannot stack their cosmo into a single champion — the receiving vessel can only hold so much.

### Not by the same Attack — Feat 18 ✎

**Traits** Saint, concentrate. No technique defeats a Gold Saint a second time. The first strike teaches you; the second finds you ready. Your cosmo reads an attack's structure the instant it lands and rebuilds you against it. 

> You automatically adapt to harmful effects you have already experienced. The **first time** each day a given creature deals damage to you with a specific attack, spell, or effect, note it. **Every subsequent time that same effect from any source targets you**, you gain a **\+2 circumstance bonus to your AC and saving throw against it**, and you gain **resistance equal to your level** to its damage. You track adaptations for up to **five distinct effects** at once; if a sixth would be added, the oldest is forgotten. An "effect" is a specific attack routine, not a damage type — a particular monster's claw Strike, a specific spell, a named technique. The GM adjudicates what counts as "the same" attack; reskinned or fundamentally altered versions do not. 

> **Adapting mid-strike.** Once per round, when you would be damaged by an effect you have already adapted to, you may use a **reaction** to heighten your adaptation: until the start of your next turn, your resistance to that effect increases to **twice your level**, and if the effect required a save, you improve your degree of success by one step.

> **Special** On any day your Cloth's constellation is **ascendant** (or Exalted), your cosmo reads faster than experience: you adapt to an effect the **first** time it damages you, rather than needing to have suffered it once before.  
> .  
>

## Level 20

### Athena Exclamation — Feat 20

**Traits** Saint, concentrate, uncommon The forbidden technique. Three cosmos ignited as one tear open a hole where the world used to be.

> **Requirements** You are within 30 feet of at least **two other creatures** who have this feat (Saints, or beings the GM rules can channel cosmo at this tier), and all three of you spend the actions together. **Athena Exclamation** ✦✦✦ (three actions, and requires two allies to each spend 3 actions) Choose a point within **100 feet**. Every creature in a **60-foot burst** around that point takes **30d6 force damage** (basic Reflex save against the highest Cosmo DC among the three casters). Creatures that critically fail are also knocked prone and deafened for 1 minute.  
>   
> The terrain in the area is **permanently obliterated** — reduced to a crater of fused glass and silence. Structures without saves are destroyed; the landscape does not recover.  
>   
> **Special** Using Athena Exclamation is regarded in the Sanctuary as the gravest taboo a Saint can commit — it is the technique that killed gods and drowned continents. Every Saint NPC who learns you have used it will treat you accordingly, and the GM is encouraged to make its use a story event, not a combat option. Each participant is **drained 2** afterward, recoverable only by a full day's rest under an open sky.

### Beyond the Eighth — Feat 20 ✎

**Traits** Saint Your consciousness has already crossed death once. Now it lingers on the far shore at will.

> Your **Arayashiki** (Eighth Sense, level 19\) improves in three ways:  
> 

> - Its duration increases from 1 minute to **10 minutes** (or until the encounter ends).  
> - While Arayashiki is active, you are no longer limited to acting *as if* alive — you may **travel to and from the edge of death as movement**: once per round you may Stride through any barrier as though it were not there (you briefly step into the space between life and death), provided you begin and end in an open space.  
> - When Arayashiki ends and you would die, you may instead choose to **return changed**: you drop to dying 3 rather than dying, and your Cloth shatters, but you are not beyond restoration. You can benefit from this reprieve only **once**; the second time your Eighth Sense carries you past death, it keeps you there.

>   
> *(This is the ability that lets a Gold Saint walk out of the underworld. Use it as the campaign's punctuation, not its grammar.)*  
> 

### Constellation of One — Feat 20

**Traits** Saint, uncommon The sky is not finished. It has been added to before — every constellation was drawn by someone, and the ones with the most stars belonged to the ones who did the most. What you have done is enough that the sky has made room for it. A star kindles in your constellation where there was none, and it will still be burning long after your name is a story.

> Work with your GM to identify the single deed that defines your career as a Saint — a battle survived, a person saved, a god refused, an oath kept at ruinous cost. That deed becomes a **star**, permanently added to your Cloth's constellation. Name it.  
>   
> **For you, immediately:**  
> 

> - Your **Ascendant Boon becomes permanent**. It is active every day, regardless of the sky. On days your constellation is genuinely ascendant you gain your **Zenith Boon** instead; on days it is Exalted, your Zenith Boon and one additional benefit of the GM's choosing drawn from your star's story.

>   
> **For every Saint of your constellation, forever:**  
> 

> - Any character who wears your Gold Cloth after you — an NPC successor, another player's character, a character in a **future campaign at this table** — gains the buff of your star. What your star gives may vary, it could be an special action, or a permanent passive.  
> - Once per day, the wearer may call on your star: they gain the benefit of your Cloth's **Ascendant Boon for 1 minute**, regardless of the sky.  
> - Your name is legible in the sky to anyone with Astronomy Lore. Stargazers reading that constellation see it. NPCs who study the heavens know what you did.

>   
> **Special** This is a **legacy feat**. It is designed to outlive the character who takes it, and the GM should record the star's name somewhere durable — the campaign document, the table's shared notes, a physical card kept in the game box. The next player to pick up the Cloth inherits it. That is the entire point: a Gold Saint's real capstone is not a technique, it's *being remembered by the sky*.  
>   
> *(Balance note: the permanent Ascendant Boon is deliberately enormous — it converts a 7.7%-uptime feature into a 100%-uptime one, roughly a \+30% permanent power increase against the tuning curve. At level 20, with the campaign ending, that is appropriate; it should never be available earlier. The inherited \+1 for successors is small on purpose — a legacy should be felt, not game-breaking for the next character.)*  
>

## Appendix — feats by function

**Sky manipulation** (make your day happen more often): Read the Constellation (8), Golden Cosmo (10), Second Ascension (12), Constellation of One (16), The Thirteenth (18), Two Skies (18).

**Cosmo economy** (more/faster focus & techniques): Heated-Up Cosmo (2), Cloth Bond (2), Second Cloth Technique (4), Twin Cosmo (8), Cosmo Legend (10), Zenith Reach (16).

**Survivability**: Saint's Vow (1), Rise (4), Speed of Light (14), Beyond the Eighth (20).

**Offense & utility**: Cosmo Sense (1), Pandora Box (1), Fists of the Constellation (1), Speed of Sound (6), Prophecy's Weight (6), Athena Exclamation (20).

*All feats are Saint class feats and cost no BCS points; the class budget remains 2100\.*
